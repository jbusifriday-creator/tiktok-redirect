Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Friday uploader ("the app") is a small personal tool. It is used by one person, its owner, to send the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

When the owner logs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token for the owner's account with the user.info.basic and video.upload permissions. It uses them only to upload video files to the owner's TikTok inbox.

What the app stores

  • The login tokens, stored on the owner's private server and never shared.
  • A log of which videos were uploaded and their status.

The app does not collect, store, or share personal data about anyone other than its owner. It does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising, and it does not sell or share data with third parties.

Retention and deletion

Tokens and logs are kept until the owner deletes them. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services, which makes the stored tokens stop working.
